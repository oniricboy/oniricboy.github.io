oniricboy is a visual artist interested in the space between what’s real and what just feels real. Through comics, characters, and personal projects, his work explores memory, identity, and those moments where something feels… off. Not everything needs to make sense. Sometimes it’s enough to feel like the world just blinked, and wonder if you’re still dreaming or if you just forgot you woke up.
